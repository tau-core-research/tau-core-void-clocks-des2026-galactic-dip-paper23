Please find enclosed the revised manuscript (v7.0),

  "Void-Volume-Weighted Optical Distances Confronting DESI BAO, Compressed Planck CMB,
   and Four Type Ia Supernova Compilations"

by Jozsef Olcsak, prepared as a regular research article for JCAP, Physical Review D, or
The Open Journal of Astrophysics.

Following constructive referee feedback on v6.0, this v7.0 revision streamlines the paper
into a focused, self-contained cosmological analysis and incorporates four major empirical
and methodological upgrades:

1. Honest k = 5 Parameter Accounting & Restrained Academic Framing:
   - Galactic disk-kinematics material and external algebraic coupling priors have been
     completely separated out so this manuscript focuses strictly on late-time cosmological
     distances with standard academic prose.
   - Because p = 1 void-volume weighting is adopted after redshift-independent weighting
     (p = 0) fails CMB + BAO constraints, H2-vol and Forward H2-Sachs are penalized
     throughout as k = 5 models (one free parameter alpha beyond k = 4 flat LCDM, and one
     fewer than k = 6 CPL), with post-data model-selection caveats stated explicitly.

2. Full Compressed Planck 2018 CMB Priors & Explicit H_0 / f_{v,0} Sensitivity:
   - Replaces the single theta_* prior with the full marginalized 3x3 Planck 2018
     TT,TE,EE+lowE MCMC covariance on (omega_b, omega_c, 100*theta_*), cross-checked
     independently against the Chen, Huang & Wang (2019, JCAP 02, 028) (R, l_A, omega_b)
     distance prior.
   - Explicitly documents common-H_0 vs. bare-Hbar_0 normalization and demonstrates
     robustness across fixed f_{v,0} in [0.72, 0.80] (including Wiltshire's 0.762 benchmark)
     without requiring transition-matching calibration H1.

3. Multi-Compilation Concordance Across Pantheon+, DES-Dovekie, DES-SN5YR, and Union3:
   - Bundling and fitting all four public SN Ia datasets jointly with DESI BAO and Planck
     2018 priors yields mutually consistent mixing weights:
       * Pantheon+:   alpha_hat = 0.422 +- 0.127 (Delta_chi^2 = -10.96, Delta_BIC = -3.59)
       * DES-Dovekie: alpha_hat = 0.432 +- 0.122 (Delta_chi^2 = -12.66, Delta_BIC = -5.14)
       * DES-SN5YR:   alpha_hat = 0.545 +- 0.121 (Delta_chi^2 = -20.30, Delta_BIC = -12.78)
       * Union3:      alpha_hat = 0.594 +- 0.141 (Delta_chi^2 = -15.15, Delta_BIC = -11.51)
     matching 2-parameter CPL in total chi^2 within <= 2.1 on every sample (Delta_BIC =
     -3.76 to -9.51 relative to CPL) and reproducing the DES-Dovekie + DESI + Planck CPL
     result (-0.818 +- 0.057, -0.644 +- 0.228) directly from public data.

4. Direct 3D Cosmic-Void Sightline Ray-Tracing Test (Pantheon+ x SDSS DR7 VoidFinder):
   - Cross-correlating N = 311 Pantheon+ sightlines through the 39,735 3D VoidFinder
     spheres of Douglass, Veyrat & BenZvi (2023, ApJS 265, 7) yields a z-detrended GLS
     slope d(Delta_mu)/d(f_{v,LOS}) = -0.058 +- 0.036 mag (-1.59 sigma), matching the
     negative sign predicted by faster void clocks while remaining statistically consistent
     with both the small z <= 0.105 single-shell expectation (-0.007 to -0.014 mag) and
     noise, and establishing a -0.053 mag (> 5 sigma) forecast for DESI + Euclid/LSST.
